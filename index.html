Key rules to actually get results

Stop skipping breakfast. Eating protein in the morning reduces cravings for the rest of the day. It's one of the easiest fat loss wins and costs almost no effort.

Cut the soda completely — diet or regular. Swap for sparkling water, black coffee, or plain water. This alone can remove 200–400 calories a day without changing anything else.

Sleep 7–8 hours. Fat loss genuinely stalls without it. Cortisol goes up, hunger hormones spike, and workouts get worse. Sleep is part of the plan.

Drink 2–3 liters of water daily. Thirst is frequently mistaken for hunger, especially in the afternoon. Most people are chronically slightly dehydrated.

Don't weigh yourself every day. Weight swings 2–4 lbs daily based on water, food, and hormones. Take progress photos every 2–3 weeks — that's your real feedback.

Progressive overload. The way muscle gets built is by doing slightly more over time — one more rep, slightly heavier weight — every 1–2 weeks on your main lifts. If the weight never changes, the body never changes.

Consistency over perfection. A missed day or a bad meal doesn't matter. Missing a week matters. Show up even when you're not feeling it — the mood follows the motion.
